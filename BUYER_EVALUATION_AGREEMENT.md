# Zebepay Buyer Evaluation Agreement

This agreement is a buyer-review boundary for Zebepay private commercial
source-code evaluation.

## Evaluation Scope

Approved evaluators may review Zebepay only to assess a possible commercial
source-code purchase, setup engagement, white-label customization, or supported
handoff.

Evaluation access may include:

- Private GitHub read-only access.
- Product README and buyer documentation.
- Architecture, API, setup, deployment, and release notes.
- Screenshots, sales materials, and commercial positioning documents.
- Time-limited review access approved by MD.

Evaluation access does not include:

- Ownership transfer.
- Resale rights.
- Redistribution rights.
- Public publishing rights.
- Production-use rights.
- Banking, payment, legal, compliance, or security certification.
- Any right to claim CBN, NIBSS, bank, or payment-provider approval.

## Confidentiality

The evaluator must keep source code, documentation, screenshots, credentials,
deployment notes, pricing discussions, and commercial terms confidential unless
MD gives written approval.

## Source-Code Protection

The evaluator must not copy, publish, share, mirror, scrape, train on, resell,
or disclose Zebepay source code or private buyer materials outside the approved
review team.

## Commercial Boundary

Zebepay is sold as a private commercial source-code foundation. It is not
public open-source software and is not a live regulated payment processor.

## Go-Live Boundary

The evaluator or buyer is responsible for all licensing, regulated provider
contracts, KYC/AML setup, production security hardening, independent audit,
legal review, monitoring, and final go-live approval.

## Access End

Evaluation access may be removed after the approved review period, after a
failed sale, after breach of confidentiality, or at MD direction.
