# Zebepay Pricing And Offer

Zebepay is open for commercial sale with flexible pricing.

The seller may accept any approved price depending on the buyer, license type, deployment scope, customization request, and support requirement.

## Offer Types

## 1. Source License

Buyer receives controlled access to the Zebepay codebase or a packaged release.

Suggested use:

- developers
- agencies
- technical founders
- internal product teams

Price: custom.

## 2. Source License Plus Setup

Buyer receives the Zebepay codebase or packaged release plus setup guidance.

Suggested use:

- founders who need installation help
- small teams without full technical capacity
- businesses that want faster setup

Price: custom.

## 3. Deployment Support

Buyer receives Zebepay plus support for deployment planning and launch preparation.

Suggested use:

- commercial operators
- payment/wallet startups
- agencies delivering to clients

Price: custom.

## 4. White-Label Customization

Buyer receives Zebepay customized for their own brand, market, or workflow.

Suggested use:

- private-label fintech products
- client projects
- enterprise buyers

Price: custom.

## Buyer-Facing Pricing Statement

Zebepay is available for private commercial purchase. Pricing is flexible and agreed based on what the buyer needs: source access, setup help, deployment support, or white-label customization.

## Internal Pricing Rule

Do not publish a fixed price unless MD approves one. Use flexible pricing until a buyer offer or sales strategy is confirmed.
