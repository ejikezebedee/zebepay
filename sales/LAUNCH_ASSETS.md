Zebepay is now packaged for buyer review: a Nigerian banking and wallet infrastructure foundation for fintech founders, agencies, cooperatives, and licensed operators.

It includes customer banking, admin operations, backend API, shared banking primitives, PostgreSQL schema, NGN/kobo handling, KYC-ready fields, ledger and transfer workflows, risk review, notifications, audit boundaries, and setup/deployment/API documentation.

Software only. Buyers must provide licensing, regulated partners, compliance approval, provider integrations, and production security review before going live.

## Long Launch Post

Most fintech templates look good but leave the hard banking foundation untouched.

Zebepay is built as a serious Nigerian fintech source-code foundation. It includes a customer banking app, admin operations dashboard, backend API, shared banking package, PostgreSQL-compatible schema, NGN/kobo money handling, Nigerian bank-code support, BVN/NIN-ready KYC fields, ledger architecture, transfer risk controls, notification workflows, and audit boundaries.

The package also includes setup, deployment, API, troubleshooting, buyer handoff, security, release, and sales documentation so a buyer can evaluate and extend the product with fewer unknowns.

It is not a licensed bank or regulated payment processor. Buyers must connect approved providers, complete legal/compliance review, run a production security audit, and obtain all required approvals before live use.
